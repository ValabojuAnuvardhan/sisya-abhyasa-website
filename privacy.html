# SISYA ABHYASA — Privacy Notice **Last updated: 1 November 2026** ## 1. Introduction SISYA ABHYASA is an early-stage student development platform being developed to connect learning, engineering practice, collaboration and career preparation. We respect your privacy and aim to handle your personal information responsibly. This notice explains what information we collect through our early-access registration form and how we use it. ## 2. Information We Collect When you submit our early-access form, we may collect: - **Name:** To identify your registration. - **Email address:** To respond to your enquiry and communicate with you. - **Role:** To understand whether you are a student, developer, mentor, educator, recruiter or another participant. - **Area of interest:** To understand whether you are interested in early access, feedback, contributing, mentoring or other activities. - **Optional message:** Any additional information you choose to share. Please do not submit passwords, financial information, government identification numbers or other sensitive personal information through this form. ## 3. How We Use Your Information We use the information submitted through the form to: - Respond to enquiries and expressions of interest. - Contact people interested in SISYA ABHYASA. - Understand student and community needs. - Gather product feedback and suggestions. - Explore potential collaboration and mentoring opportunities. We do not use this early-access form to create student accounts. ## 4. How Information Is Processed Our public website is hosted using Netlify. Form submissions are processed through Netlify Forms and notification emails are sent to our designated SISYA ABHYASA email inbox. Your information may therefore be processed by these service providers to operate the registration process. ## 5. Data Sharing We do not intend to sell personal information collected through this form. Access to submitted information should be limited to people who need it to respond to enquiries and manage early-access registrations. We may disclose information where required by applicable law. ## 6. Data Retention We aim to retain registration information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this notice or to meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of your registration information. ## 7. Your Choices and Requests You may contact us to: - Ask what registration information you have submitted. - Request correction of inaccurate information. - Request deletion of your registration information. - Withdraw your interest in receiving further communications. We will review and respond to reasonable requests. ## 8. Children's Privacy This early-access form is not intended to collect sensitive personal information from children. If you are under the age of 18, please involve a parent or guardian before submitting your information. ## 9. Changes to This Notice We may update this notice as SISYA ABHYASA develops. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date. ## 10.
